Mainframe

Mainframe is a personal assistant tool built by Mark Pot for his own use. It connects to his own Google account to read and summarize his email, send him a daily morning briefing (De Ochtendpost) and help with his calendar, documents and to-dos.

It is not a public service. Only the owner's own Google account uses it.
