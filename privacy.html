Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Who uses this app

Mainframe is a personal tool used only by its owner, Mark Pot, with his own Google account. It is not offered to other people.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission, Mainframe accesses his Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Forms, Tasks and Contacts, only to do what he asks: for example reading recent email to write a daily briefing, sending that briefing to himself, and managing his own files and appointments.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is processed on the owner's own computer and in his own cloud functions (Modal).
  • To summarize email, short excerpts may be sent to an AI model (via OpenRouter). Financial emails are excluded from this.
  • Login tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and in his own private cloud secret store.
  • Data is never sold, shared with third parties for advertising, or used for any purpose other than the owner's own use.

Google API Services

Mainframe's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time via myaccount.google.com/permissions.
